Genres

MUSICAL FAMILIES

Rock

A broad family of popular music rooted in earlier traditions including rhythm and blues, country, and rock and roll.

Electric · Guitar · Band

Jazz

A diverse tradition strongly associated with improvisation, rhythmic complexity, blues traditions, and evolving ensemble forms.

Improvisation · Swing · Harmony

Hip-Hop

A cultural and musical movement encompassing rap, DJing, sampling, breakdancing, graffiti, and diverse regional traditions.

Rap · Sampling · Rhythm

Electronic

Music involving electronic technologies for sound creation, processing, sequencing, performance, or production.

Synthesis · Production · Texture

Classical

A broad category covering multiple traditions of composed Western art music across centuries.

Composition · Orchestra · Form

Folk

Music connected to cultural traditions, communities, storytelling, oral transmission, and evolving popular forms.

Tradition · Storytelling · Culture
