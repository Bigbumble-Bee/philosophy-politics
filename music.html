MUSIC HISTORY

A timeline of sound

Recording technology, social change, migration, politics, and culture all changed the way music was made and heard.

1877–1920s

Early recording

Mechanical recording transformed music from something primarily experienced in person into something that could be stored and replayed.

1920s–1940s

Jazz & the radio age

Jazz developed alongside new recording and broadcasting technologies, while radio brought music into homes on a mass scale.

1950s–1970s

Rock & popular music

Rock and roll, soul, folk, funk, and other movements reshaped youth culture, recording, performance, and popular identity.

1970s–1990s

Hip-hop & sampling

Hip-hop developed new approaches to rhythm, DJing, sampling, storytelling, production, and community expression.

2000s–present

The digital era

Downloads, streaming, social media, home production, and algorithmic discovery changed how artists release music and how listeners find it.
