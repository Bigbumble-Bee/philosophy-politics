Types of notes

NOT EVERYTHING BELONGS IN THE SAME NOTEBOOK
Quick

Quick notes

A place to capture an idea before it disappears. Don't worry about organization yet.

Study

Study notes

Organized explanations of material you're actively learning.

Research

Research notes

Evidence, sources, arguments, interpretations, and conclusions gathered while investigating a question.

Cornell

Cornell notes

Notes divided into cues, main notes, and a summary to encourage active recall and review.

Ideas

Idea notebook

Essay ideas, theories, projects, creative concepts, questions, and connections.

Archive

Reference notes

Information you expect to return to repeatedly: definitions, timelines, frameworks, and important facts.
